## 6. Calibration / validation / holdout roles

### Calibration

Purpose: derive candidate parameter values under a previously frozen method and version regime.

Selection semantics:

- prospective boundaries;
- no outcome-driven backdating;
- organic durable SHADOW aggregates only for maturity;
- preserve unobserved calendar gaps;
- do not pool incompatible runtime/policy/evidence regimes.

### Validation

Purpose: test the frozen candidate against later unseen organic Production evidence.

Rules:

- non-overlapping with calibration;
- strictly later;
- no retuning on validation;
- failed validation does not become calibration evidence retroactively under the same candidate version.

### Holdout

Purpose: sequestered confirmatory evidence for promotion of a validated candidate into a successor sufficiency policy.

Rules:

- non-overlapping with calibration and validation;
- strictly later than validation;
- no tuning against holdout;
- after failed validation/tuning, a fresh holdout is required for the next versioned candidate.

The split sizes and dates are intentionally not assigned in 9O.

## 7. Version partitioning

A calibration regime is keyed by at least:

- activation version
- policy contract version
- runtime version
- evidence schema version
- context bucket version
- activation scope

Incompatible regimes cannot be pooled merely to increase volume.

A change begins a new regime unless a separate governance decision proves compatibility for the exact calibration purpose.

## 8. Parameter methodology registry

### minimum_observation_horizon

Question: at what prospective observation boundary does naturally recurring evidence become informative enough for calibration?

Method family: ordered temporal-prefix accumulation plus later-window replication.

Eligible evidence: organic daily aggregates in one compatible version regime.

Forbidden substitute: controlled/synthetic volume, operator-chosen calendar heuristic.

Insufficient data: `AWAIT_MORE_ORGANIC_EVIDENCE`.

### minimum_organic_execution_volume

Question: at what accumulated organic exposure boundary do governed descriptors reproduce in later unseen evidence?

Method family: cumulative organic exposure frontier plus out-of-sample replication.

No execution count is assigned in 9O.

### required_temporal_recurrence

Question: how should burst-concentrated evidence be distinguished from naturally recurring operation?

Method family: ordered UTC-daily recurrence-pattern analysis with natural gaps retained.

No day/week/recurrence count is assigned.

### required_context_breadth

Question: when do privacy-safe marginals show reproducible breadth?

Method family: independent marginal support/distribution replication for:

- PRIMARY_CONCERN_CLASS
- SENSITIVITY_RISK_CLASS
- CONCERN_STRUCTURE_CLASS
- SURVEY_COMPLETENESS_CLASS
- RECENT_INSTABILITY_CLASS

Forbidden: marginal joining, pseudo-user reconstruction, unique-user inference.

### required_safety_relevant_branch_coverage

Question: what organic observation of CAUTION/RESTRICT/DEFER is enough for maturity?

Method family: organic action occurrence/recurrence; controlled/synthetic evidence can only establish reachability and deterministic logic.

Unresolved dependency:

`SAFETY_RELEVANT_BRANCH_COVERAGE_ACCEPTANCE_OBJECTIVE`

Organic frequency alone cannot define the acceptable coverage target.

### unknown_source_tolerance

Method family: organic UNKNOWN prevalence and temporal-clustering characterization while keeping provenance separate.

Unresolved dependency:

`UNKNOWN_SOURCE_ACCEPTABILITY_OBJECTIVE`

Observed UNKNOWN prevalence describes Production; it does not itself define acceptable tolerance.

### runtime_error_tolerance

Method family: organic runtime-error prevalence, recurrence, and clustering characterization.

Unresolved dependency:

`RUNTIME_ERROR_RISK_ACCEPTANCE_OBJECTIVE`

A single observed event is not automatically systematic instability, but an empirical frequency alone does not establish acceptable risk.

### fallback_tolerance

Method family: organic fallback prevalence, recurrence, and clustering characterization.

Unresolved dependency:

`FALLBACK_RISK_ACCEPTANCE_OBJECTIVE`

### stability_criterion

Hard stability gates:

- runtime version identity
- policy contract identity
- activation scope identity
- evidence collection semantics identity

These are governance/integrity properties.

Empirical outcome-distribution stability is separate and requires:

`EMPIRICAL_OUTCOME_DRIFT_ACCEPTANCE_OBJECTIVE`

The descriptive distribution can be measured without an arbitrary threshold; deciding how much drift is acceptable cannot.
