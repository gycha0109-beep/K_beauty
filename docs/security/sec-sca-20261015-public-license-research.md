DES web retrieval initially failed through one tool; a direct HTTPS fetch succeeded. Raw usage/code HTML SHA256 values observed were `17eead5e3220b00cc5e37c8d07d7b07fd078f865dad98f324f963446a9d0f265` and `4653dbb1fdfec921866356bc4aaa000f515c4870785f216747190edd06476d21`. The repo carries the human-readable authoritative licensing statement and full source notice, with their own hashes; raw HTML with advertising scripts is not a runtime dependency or redistributed code. The facts come from the author's public page, not from assuming an issue reporter can grant rights.

The SourceForge WebRSA download page advertises webrsa-1.0.tar.gz SHA256 `d64c580201891195236a7942f16a86ee6bc7a053d23abef7d2ffeb63178e9343`. Earlier attempts returned HTML/nonmatching bytes and were correctly rejected. The subsequent exact path `/project/webrsa/webrsa/webrsa-1.0/webrsa-1.0.tar.gz` succeeded over HTTPS on four official mirrors: downloads, master, netix and pilotfiber. All returned identical 28,732-byte archives matching the published digest before parsing. The nine regular release files have no standalone LICENSE/COPYING/NOTICE; the original OAEP header and README both specify BSD without full conditions. Thus the original has now been recovered, while the precise BSD variant remains open. See [archive inventory and original header](sec-sca-20261015-oaep-original-recovery.json).

The official [CVS download](https://sourceforge.net/p/webrsa/code/) also supplied a valid 61,151-byte snapshot (observed SHA256 `60429fc7a7818df5d07358c1c5379ffdf22512895dabf587410bb8421dece300`). Selected original RCS sources corroborate the 2003 author/date/grant and the historic license hyperlink to SourceForge category187, now404. No independent published digest was found for the snapshot, so it is supporting evidence rather than a separately authenticated release. CVS administration files are excluded from published evidence. Neither archive was executed or adopted into the app. The existing candidate packet remains frozen; this supplementary research does not silently change its digest or mark final rights approved.

The [Debian0.8.1 copyright review](https://sources.debian.org/src/node-node-forge/0.8.1~dfsg-1%2Bdeb10u1/debian/copyright/) classifies pkcs1.js as BSD-3-Clause plus Expat~Intact, but explicitly assumes top-level licensing generally applies. It is evidence of another distributor's interpretation, not the original rightsholder's missing license text or binding legal authority. It does not by itself close the remaining OAEP variant assessment.

Existing Forge issue382 records historical header/minification difficulty; that does not waive notice delivery. Issue1032 has a regular user's general BSD comment, not a maintainer's rights grant. These sources are distinguished from primary permission texts.

## External obligations versus internal assurance

- External conditions: preserve actually applicable copyright/permission/conditions/disclaimers; comply with BSD no-endorsement restrictions where applicable; supply required notices with distributed binaries/source. These are not weakened.
- Internal assurance: a second human, exact historical line mapping and an individual author reply are not conditions found in the examined licenses. They must not be invented as mandatory legal gates. Useful unresolved lineage information remains visible in `informationalGaps`.
- Open applicable terms: unknown material license conditions cannot be waived by relabeling them informational. OAEP's exact conditions remain explicitly open for the owner's final evidence-based assessment; current actual redistribution/upload approvals remain false.
- Human decision: the sole project owner gives the final project legal/redistribution decision, using the evidence and optional advice. Codex/CI validates bytes and signed scope; it does not certify ownership or substitute a fabricated legal judgment. Only a genuinely material unresolvable rights issue should trigger a new decision request with evidence and alternatives.

V2 records `rightsReview.upstreamReplyRequired=false`, an array of disclosed `informationalGaps`, and a separate `unresolved` list for material unmet external conditions. Disclosed internal gaps do not alone block admission. Missing external conditions, unsigned/fake approvals, revoked keys, missing real upload/final-redistribution authorization, unsafe targets and audit exceptions still fail closed.

## Browser notice delivery preparation

`sca-release-browser-notices.mjs` stages a new local-only directory from the exact verified patched Forge tarball. It colocates all six frozen browser files, unchanged Forge LICENSE, full combined third-party notices, the supplemental SBOM/review findings and a SHA256 manifest. A same-size notice mutation and a missing NOTICE must fail verification. Both clean runners reconstruct the kit and compare the manifest digest. This addresses the preparation of standalone browser notice delivery without editing browser code.

This kit is **not** a published bundle, installed private-registry package, final license decision or proof of live recipient delivery. The kit is temporary and deleted with the isolated graph. Actual upload/readback and final distribution packaging must still demonstrate the complete materials reach the intended recipient. No expiring artifact or temporary file path enters the product lockfile.
